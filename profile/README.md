# CasePulse

**The client portal for law firms.**

CasePulse is a white-labeled client portal and mobile app for law firms. It's the client-facing layer on top of the case management system a firm already runs, not a case management system itself. Clients see where their case stands, message the firm, exchange files, and complete forms, all under the firm's own brand, while staff keep working in the system they know.

## Where we came from

We started by building custom client portals for individual law firms across the country. Needles firms were where it began, and the problem was always the same: a busy firm, and a client asking "what's happening with my case?" In 2017 we turned what we'd learned into CasePulse, so any firm could add a portal without the cost or time of custom development.

## How we work

- **We enhance the tools firms already use.** Nothing gets replaced. Staff never learn a second system or log in anywhere new.
- **You get us.** When you partner with CasePulse, you're not just getting software. Setup, training, and support come directly from our team.
- **We don't lock firms in.** Plans are month-to-month with no long-term contract.
